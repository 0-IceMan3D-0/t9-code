# Home screen widgets (Android)

The Android app has ten home screen widgets. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, choose **Widgets**, and find them under T3² Code. Every widget can be resized; smaller sizes show less, such as one thread or a single line. **New thread bar**, **Gauges**, **Status line**, **Limits** and **Tokens today** are one row high. Every widget sits on a plain rounded card that follows your phone's light or dark mode. On Android 12 and newer, the card takes its corners and neutral colors from your launcher and wallpaper, so it matches your other widgets. To drop the card from a widget, turn it on under **Settings → Appearance → Transparent widgets**; it then shows white text with a soft shadow straight on your wallpaper. They show the app icon you chose in **Settings → Appearance**.

## Keeping widgets up to date

The server, quick action and new thread bar widgets update on their own, as do the server parts of **Gauges** and **Status line**. Thread, usage and limit figures come from the app, so they stay current while the app is open or keeps running in the background: while the built-in server runs, or with **Stay connected** on in **Settings → Notifications**. Otherwise Android pauses the app soon after you leave it, and these widgets show **Updated … ago** until you open the app again. **Limits** keeps showing the last reading, and shows a window as full once its reset time passes. The same note appears when a server they list is not connected.

Widgets only redraw when something they show changes, so they do not wake your phone or use battery while nothing happens. Memory use refreshes about every two minutes while the screen is on.

If you force-stop the app in Android settings, the server widget may keep showing the old state until you tap it or open the app.

The **Subscription usage** widget is described in [Usage and limits](./usage.md#subscription-usage-widget). On Android it needs Android 12L or newer.
